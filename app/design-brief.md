# FLORE | Floral Business OS

- Design read: FLORE is an operating system for independent florists who need to run a beautiful, precise and profitable business without losing the human craft behind each arrangement.
- Concept spine: The living atelier. Every operational signal feels like a botanical specimen organized in a calm, intelligent studio.
- Delivery tier: cinema, with a scroll-scrubbed botanical film and a practical operations workspace.
- Animation mode: animated-website
- Palette: deep botanical green #173B32, midnight evergreen #102A25, mineral mint #B7D5C3, soft chalk #F2F4EE, muted sage #8FA79A. One accent: mineral mint.
- Typography: Outfit for display and interface, IBM Plex Mono for labels and data. No serif.
- Journey shape: single-shot. One continuous macro-to-wide journey through a floral atelier, from stem detail to arranged bouquet to a calm worktable.
- Journey: 1) Materia viva: close detail of fresh petals and stems. 2) El taller: hands arranging stems and wrapping paper. 3) El pedido: finished bouquet and order card. 4) El negocio: organized worktable with flowers, ledger and packaging.
- World grammar: cinematic botanical editorial, real tactile surfaces, forest green and mineral mint palette, morning side light from upper left, no text or logos in the film.
- Mobile framing: central flower subjects, vertical crop safe, all focal points within center 60%.
- Section plan: dashboard overview (asymmetric workspace), order queue (table), inventory (split stock list and alerts), CRM (customer ledger), sales and finance (chart + transactions), content studio (editorial rows), social listening (signal feed), analytics (metric grid + insight panel), settings (form).
- Asset plan: procedural botanical film and exact frame posters due visual generation service plan restriction; brand wordmark and favicon authored locally; dashboard uses charts, tables and interface components rather than decorative stock imagery.
- CTA inventory: Create order, Add product, Add customer, Record sale, Save settings. Each has a distinct role and placement.
